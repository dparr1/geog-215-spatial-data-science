Points from the chapter quizzes. Every answer, re-asks included, is +100 if right and −100 if wrong. A right answer also earns a streak bonus of +10 for each right answer in a row just before it in the same quiz, so a run of right answers scores 100, 110, 120, 130, and so on. A wrong answer resets the streak, and every new quiz starts from zero. A student's total adds up every quiz they have taken; each chapter column is the total for that chapter's quizzes. Best streak is the most right answers in a row in any one quiz. Quizzes taken before scoring was added count 100 per right answer and −100 per wrong one, with no streak bonus.

The quiz page after each chapter asks 10 random questions, then re-asks any a student missed until all are right. Correct and incorrect count every answer, re-asks included, and success is correct answers as a share of all answers. Best first try is the highest share of a finished quiz's questions a student got right the first time.
